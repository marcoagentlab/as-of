# Disclaimer

As-Of (RWA Normalized API, schema v0) publishes **informational** data.

- Not investment advice.
- Not an offer, solicitation, or recommendation to buy or sell any security, token, or other instrument.
- Not a Korea STO engine, trading venue, custodian, or transfer agent.
- Eligibility responses are **docs-level rules**. They are not a live `can_transfer` simulation and not a legal determination of who may hold an asset.
- Every figure is supposed to carry a `source` and `as_of`. If a number cannot be sourced, it is `null` with `quality: unavailable`. Unknown is preferred to wrong.
- `estimated` means a human-curated structural field (issuer, wrapper, public eligibility shape). It is not a verified mark.
- `verified` is reserved for sourced figures. v0 seeds never use it.
